The City of Bristol utilizes the Connecticut Purchasing Group to post Request for Proposal (RFP) and bid opportunities. The City will continue using the current procurement process and will fully transition to the Connecticut Purchasing Group in November 2025. At this time, all solicitations will be published on the platform.

Registering is free; follow the registration path in the link below and select the "Limited Access" option. You will receive notifications of matching solicitations and addenda even when choosing the Free, Limited Access package. To receive notifications about Statewide or Federal opportunities, select one of the other registration options or call BidNet's Support Team at 800-835-4603 option 2 for assistance.
