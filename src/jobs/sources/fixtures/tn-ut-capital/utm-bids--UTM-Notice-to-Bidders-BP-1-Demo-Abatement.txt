UTM New Residence Hall
University of Tennessee Martin
Martin, TN
UT Project #999/011-104-2024
Bid Package 1 (BP-1) – Abatement and Demolition of Browning Hall
Advertisement for Bidding, March 4, 2026
Hardaway Construction invites all qualified vendors to submit complete bids for the above
referenced project.
BP-1 consists of full & complete abatement and demolition of the existing Browning Hall building
to make way for a new residence hall.
Bid Package documents will be made available through Building Connected by Hardaway
invitation to responded bidders.
Complete bids are to be emailed to Andy Brunson at abrunson@hardaway.net and Andrew Enos
at aenos@hardaway.net no later than 2:00 CST on Wednesday April 1, 2026. Please do not send
mail or telephone bids. Following the bid date there will be a two-week review process by
Hardaway prior to making an un-o(icial award. Contract issuance to the awarded subcontractor
will be approximately May 1 and will be contingent on Hardaway’s execution date of the
Construction Contract with the State.
Bid Bond is not required for this bid submission.
All bidders must be licensed and insured in the State of Tennessee for their respective trade(s) of
work applicable to this project.
It is the express desire of the University of Tennessee and the State Building Commission to
include an emphasis on diversity in its contractual relationships with contractors for the
construction and renovations of State projects under the jurisdiction of the Commission.
