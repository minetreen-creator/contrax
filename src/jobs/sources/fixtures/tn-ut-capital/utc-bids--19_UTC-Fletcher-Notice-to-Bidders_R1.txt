NOTICE TO BIDDERS
University of Tennessee Chattanooga – Fletcher Hall Addition & Renovation
SBC Project #540/005-02-2023 (Bid Package 4 – Main Building Package)
Request for Bidding
Dated: January 28, 2026
Hoar Construction will be accepting complete bids from pre-qualified bidders for the work in Bid
Package 4.
Complete bids are to be emailed to Hoar Construction no later than 2:00 P.M., EST, Tuesday
February 24, 2026.
Bid Package 4 consists of scopes not bid in previous bid package. The scopes included in Bid
Package 4 include, but are not limited to exterior enclosure, interior finishes, building systems, and
site furnishings / improvements.
Bid Documents include:
• Project Drawings
• Project Manual
• Bid Form
• Previous Bid Package Documents (For Reference Only)
• Project Schedule & Logistics Plan
Access to the bid documents can be requested by contacting Zackary Howe via email at
zhowe@hoar.com. Documents will be available on Wed January 28th, 2026.
A Pre-Bid Meeting will be held on Wednesday Feb 11th, 2026, at 10:00 AM EST. Pre-Bid meeting
invitations will be released to all bidders in advance of the meeting.
No bid bonds are required. A 100% Payment & Performance bond may be required. All bidders
must be licensed and qualified in the State of Tennessee.
It is the express desire of the University of Tennessee and the State Building Commission to include
an emphasis on diversity in its contractual relationships with contractors for the construction and
renovation of State projects under the jurisdiction of the Commission. The Commission
acknowledges that firms who demonstrate and embrace diversity within their programs and
policies are assisting the State in achieving its goals in building a more reflective marketplace of
the community within this State.
