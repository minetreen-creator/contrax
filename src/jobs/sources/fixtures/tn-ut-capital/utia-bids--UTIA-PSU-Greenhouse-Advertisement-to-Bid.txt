BID PROCESS AND CONTRACTOR EXPECTATIONS:
Bidding documents will be available from the CM/GC via Christman’s BuildingConnected Project Site.
Submit proposals via email to:
• Ethan Sanders, Project Manager – ethan.sanders@christmanco.com
• Jeff Lumpkins, Preconstruction Manager – jeff.lumpkins@christmanco.com
Bids may be submitted on a trade contractors preferred letterhead but should align with the scope defined in
the Work Category Scope Summary document which will be posted to BuildingConnected.
There will be a virtual pre-bid conference via Microsoft Teams on Thursday, March 26th at 11:00 AM conducted
by the Construction Manager. It is recommended that trade contractors participate in the pre-bid conference.
E-mail ethan.sanders@christmanco.com to request an invite to this Teams meeting.
All questions are to be directed to the Construction Manager no later than Friday, April 10th by end of day. No
direct contact with the Owner or Architect is requested. Please send questions to Ethan Sanders.
All contractors bidding on work must be pre-qualified through Christman’s Sub Service Center (For
Subcontractors | The Christman Company), and have all information up to date.
We will host post bid interviews for the low qualified bidders immediately after bids are received. The purpose
of the interview will be to discuss the bid scope inclusions but will also focus on schedule, submittals, safety, site
utilization and unique project requirements.
All proposals submitted shall remain valid for a period of sixty (60) days after the bid date. The Owner, Architect
and Construction Manager reserve the right to waive any irregularities, reject any or all proposals, or accept any
proposal, which, in their opinion, will serve their best interest.
The Christman Company is an equal opportunity contractor which does not discriminate based on ethnicity,
gender, age or religious beliefs.
