2026 Tabulation of Bids

    This page is for the selection of the 2026 Tabulation of Bids reports from the Alabama Department of Transportation. Please read the Tips and Caution notes shown below.

    Tips:

    • You may view the Bid Tabulations as a text file or by using Adobe Acrobat (PDF icon). Regardless of the viewing method, click on the respective icon or file size to open the file to your browser, and use your browser's [Back] button to return to this screen.
    • If you want to print the Bid Tabulations, it is highly recommended to use Adobe Acrobat.
    • Adobe Acrobat is available for free from Adobe, by clicking on the icon found on the ALDOT Bid Tabulations Main Page.

    When printing remember to:

    1. Make print request in Adobe. Some browsers may put Adobe inside of their browser.
    2. Change print orientation in Adobe to Landscape.

    The Bid Tabulations for the Letting Dates shown with file type of "PDF" below have been released. You may find them by clicking on the"PDF" on the same row as the date.
