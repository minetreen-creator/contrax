Bids, RFPs, and Other Procurements


The County uses several types of procurement processes depending on the goods or services being obtained. Bids are competitive solicitations generally used when the County is seeking specific goods, services, or construction based on defined specifications. Requests for Proposals (RFPs) are used when the County is seeking solutions to a need and may evaluate proposals based on factors beyond price, such as qualifications, experience, approach, and cost. Sole Source Procurements are used when the County determines that a particular vendor or source is uniquely able to provide the required goods or services. Emergency Procurements are used when an emergency requires the County to obtain goods or services without following the normal procurement process.

Prospective vendors are responsible for regularly checking this website for updates, including changes to solicitation dates, addenda, or other amendments to procurement documents. The County is not responsible for individually notifying vendors who have downloaded solicitation documents of any changes or addenda.

Solicitations and specifications that are available for download are posted below. If a solicitation or specification is not available for download, please contact the County at (276) 634-4670, Monday through Friday, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., to request the applicable documents.
