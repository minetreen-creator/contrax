Applicants new to the Grants for Organizations (GFO) program must complete an Intent to Apply application to determine eligibility.

Organizations that did not receive GFO funding in the previous fiscal year but are interested in being considered for the next funding cycle must submit an Intent to Apply form by September 15th annually. Eligible organizations will then complete a standard application by November 15, as a current GFO applicant would.
