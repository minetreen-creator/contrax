Please note information below regarding procurement procedures and opportunities currently available for Madison County.
New Bid Solicitations are e-mailed to customers currently registered on-line to receive bid notifications and updates. To receive notification of newly posted Solicitations and updates via email you will need to register at this site. Interested bidders may view or download a copy of a Solicitation by clicking on the bid number listed in our BID Section below.
Bid Solicitation packages will be available on the Madison County website for download.
All Bids must be submitted in a sealed envelope to the Madison County Finance Department, 1981 Hollywood Boulevard, Jackson, TN 38305
