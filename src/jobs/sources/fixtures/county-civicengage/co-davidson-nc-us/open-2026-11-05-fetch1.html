The Davidson County, North Carolina, Purchasing Department is the only official release site for these invitations or any project bids that may be issued for our invitations. Please review the information below for a list of bids that are currently open.

It is the ultimate responsibility of the bidder/proposer/quoter to ensure that they have read all solicitation documents and have received and read all applicable addenda prior to bid/proposal/quote submission. Addenda can be viewed by clicking on the solicitation name. Davidson County, North Carolina, takes reasonable steps to ensure that known prospective bidders have all applicable Addenda, but it is your responsibility to ensure that you do, before you submit a bid/proposal/quote.

The Davidson County, North Carolina, Board of Commissioners reserves the right to reject any and all bids. All firms must be licensed in North Carolina.
