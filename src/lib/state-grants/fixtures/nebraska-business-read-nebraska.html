The READ Nebraska program is administered as a beneficiary grant intended to support qualified mentorship organizations with demonstrated histories of established youth programming through mentorship activities. Program funding is awarded to experienced organizations with established operational histories and demonstrated capacity to provide literacy-focused mentorship and educational-development programming. Recipients are expected to exercise reasonable discretion in determining how best to utilize grant funds to support the program activities described in the application and to foster positive student outcomes.

Grant funds may not be used for:

  • Politically related activities;
  • Fundraising activities;
  • Alcohol, tobacco or other similar products;
  • Costs incurred outside the contract period; or,
  • Other uses prohibited by law.
