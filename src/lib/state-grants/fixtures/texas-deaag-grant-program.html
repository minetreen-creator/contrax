Grant Program - Defense Economic Adjustment Assistance Grant (DEAAG)

The Defense Economic Adjustment Assistance Grant Program (DEAAG) is an infrastructure grant program designed to assist defense communities that have been positively or negatively impacted by a change or announced change by the Department of Defense. DEAAG funding is available to local municipalities, counties, defense base development authority, junior college districts and Texas State Technical College campuses, and regional planning commissions representing these communities. DEAAG funding is available to meet matching requirements for federal funding. Grants awarded may range from $50,000 to $5 million per project.
 

Download the DEAAG Application in PDF Format

The FY 27 Round of DEAAG will open on September 1, 2026. DEAAG applications will be due on or before 5 PM Friday, November 06, 2026. Grants will be awarded at the beginning of 2027. Applications received after the deadline will be ineligible for consideration. If you have difficulty submitting the application, please contact TMPC before the deadline.

Please note that all submitted applications must be 50 pages or less. Additional pages will be deleted. DEAAG scoring will be based on the following criteria: military value, project completion, dual military/community benefit, National Defense Strategy, new missions, resiliency, cost efficiency, significance of impact, and funding contributions.

For additional information regarding the Defense Economic Adjustment Assistance Grant Program, please send a message to the Texas Military Preparedness Commission.
