Arts & Culture Programs

The Iowa Artist Career Accelerator program provides grant funding, peer learning and mentorship opportunities, and business development resources to artists of all disciplines to catalyze momentum in their artistic practices and increase their professional skills and capacity.

The program is funded through an appropriation by the Iowa Legislature to the IEDA and a grant by the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.  

Eligibility

Open to Iowa Artists at least 18 years of age or older.

Application Process

Program is currently not accepting applications.
