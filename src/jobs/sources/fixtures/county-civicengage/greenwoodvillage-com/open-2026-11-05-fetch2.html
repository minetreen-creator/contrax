Open Bids, Quotes, and Proposals for Greenwood Village


On many occasions, the Village partners with outside vendors for equipment and services to achieve exceptional facilities and experiences for the customer. If you are interested in being a part of providing the Village experience, and believe you can offer excellent quality and lasting value, we invite you to take part in our bids and proposals.

To view bids and proposals, please click the following link: www.greenwoodvillage.com/bids. For all other bids, please see the information below.

If you have any questions regarding bid processes or need additional information, please contact us at bidproposals2@greenwoodvillage.com or call 303-773-0252. Please include in your e-mail inquiry, your company name, address, phone number, fax number, e-mail address, and a contact name.
