Lucas County Bid Opportunities

Welcome to the bid and contract opportunities page for Lucas County. If you are a consultant, service provider, contractor, vendor, or supplier, this is a convenient place to learn about current bidding and contract opportunities.

All current bid opportunities are available through the PB System™. PB System™ is a web-based, bid and vendor management system that makes doing business with Lucas County easier and more efficient for vendors. PB System™ provides vendors with a user friendly, accessible means to view bid opportunities and enhanced electronic communication with the county.

PB System™ allows vendors to:

  • Receive up-to-date email bid notifications based on services or products?
  • Create and maintain a vendor profile that is accessed by county purchasing agents for quotations
  • Submit electronic bids for projects via a secure connection
  • Search all county bid opportunities, addenda, bid results and awards
  • Participate in on-line question and answer sessions

Vendors interested in doing business with Lucas County must register with the PB System™. To access the Lucas County PB System™, please visit:

http://www.planetbids.com/portal/portal.cfm?CompanyID=24980

In order to access the PB System™, you must install Adobe Flash Player version 10.1 or higher.

Download and install the latest Adobe Flash Player.

Please contact the Lucas County Support Services Department with any questions at (419) 213-4519.
