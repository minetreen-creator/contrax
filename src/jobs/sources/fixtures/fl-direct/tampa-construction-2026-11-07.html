The downloadable files available on this page are in PDF format. If you do not have Adobe Reader you may download it for free from the Adobe web site.  Bids are not received electronically.

Bids for these projects must be submitted by USPS, UPS, FedEx, or other parcel services. Email the address below to confirm delivery. Bids that are submitted in person must be delivered to the First Floor Security desk.

All bids must be CLEARLY MARKED “BID for Contract: (Number and Name) from: (Bidders Name.)”

All Questions must be submitted to ContractAdministration@tampagov.net.
