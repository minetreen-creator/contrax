Important for FY278

New Mexico Arts offers a two-year funding cycle. Applicants who are awarded funding in FY28 will be allowed to submit a Renewal Application for FY29. You must have been successfully funded in FY28 to submit a Renewal Application in FY29.

All funding applications must be submitted online, excluding Folk Arts Apprenticeships.

New applicants must create a new account to apply. If you are unsure if your organization has an account in the system, please contact New Mexico Arts staff. Please do not create duplicate accounts.

New applicants must contact New Mexico Arts program staff to determine if the proposed program meets the eligibility requirements of a funding category. Organizations who are changing proposed programs should also consult with program staff to ensure eligibility

FY28 Application Q & A

Advance Review

All new applicants are required to submit an Advance Review Application. If the applicant was declared ineligible or did not receive funding in the past year or has not applied to New Mexico Arts in the past two years, an Advance Review is required and must be submitted by the published deadline of 11:59 PM MT October 23, 2026.

If an organization was required to submit an Advance Review and did not submit by the published deadline, they will not be allowed to apply for funding until the next funding cycle opens.

An Advance Review by New Mexico Arts does not guarantee funding.

Final Deadline

The FY28 application must be submitted online by the published deadline of 11:59 PM MT December 18, 2026.
Applicants will upload or provide links to all required application materials including all artistic samples (print, digital images, audio, and video samples); Income and Expense Statement; Board Member List; etc.
