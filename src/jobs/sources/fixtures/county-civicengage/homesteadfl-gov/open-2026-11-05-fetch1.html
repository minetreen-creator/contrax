The City of Homestead has partnered with OpenGov and is excited to announce our transition from a paper-based bid solicitation process to a fully automated, web-based electronic bidding and vendor management system. There is no charge to vendors wishing to sign up for OpenGov Procurement. For new bid opportunities please register OpenGov to receive notifications to learn about current bid and contract opportunities available for consultants, service providers, contractors, vendors, or suppliers.
